Your GO account and information.

This notice describes the account functionality in GO. Account sign-in and cloud storage are available only when the account service has been connected.

What is saved

Creating an account uses your name, email address or phone number, and a password managed by Supabase Auth. Your password is not saved in your fitness records. Email or SMS verification confirms the sign-in method. Your account can store the profile information you enter, such as age, height, weight, fitness goals, avatar/photo, and training preferences, along with saved workouts, meal records, daily metrics and simulation preferences.

Who can see it

You can view your own account data. Designated GO administrators can view registered users' contact details and saved profile and fitness information to manage and support the service. Administrator access is checked by the database and directory/profile views are logged. Other ordinary users cannot read your account through the account service.

Authentication and database hosting use Supabase when enabled. Email delivery and SMS verification also involve the configured delivery provider and, for SMS, your carrier. GO administrators do not see verification codes or passwords in the member directory.

Your choices

Only enter information you want to save. Optional fitness information can be left blank, and you can edit your profile and records. You can export your progress from the Progress page. Signing out removes your account's displayed workspace from this device; it does not delete your cloud account. Your browser retains the sign-in session until you sign out or it expires. You can set or change your password in Account. Email users can request a password reset link; verified link/code sign-in is also available.

Old browser-only GO records are not automatically assigned to an account. Import them only if they are yours. Anonymous site visits are not identified in the registered-user directory. Browser-only preview mode stores information in that browser and does not create a cloud account.

Deletion and support

Contact the GO site owner through the channel where you received this website to request account or data deletion. Automated self-service deletion is not currently provided. Saved cloud data remains until deleted by the service operator; there is no automatic retention schedule in this release.

The service operator should keep this notice and a direct support contact up to date before inviting public registrations.
